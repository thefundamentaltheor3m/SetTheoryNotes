\chapter{Gödel's Constructible Universe} \label{Ch4:CH}

We previewed Gödel's constructible universe at the very start of \Cref{Ch1:CH}, where we defined the operation $\D$ informally and promised to explore it in great detail. We now build it properly, using everything \Cref{Ch3:CH} has given us: satisfaction to define it, absoluteness to compute it inside transitive models, and reflection to check the axioms in it.
