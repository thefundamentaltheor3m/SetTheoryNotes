\chapter{Trees and Suslin's Problem} \label{Ch5:CH}
\thispagestyle{empty}

Suslin asked whether the real line is characterised by a condition weaker than separability, and the answer turns out to depend on the universe of sets. This chapter states the question and then develops the combinatorics that bears on it: trees, the tree property, and the principle $\Diamond$, from which we construct a Suslin tree.
